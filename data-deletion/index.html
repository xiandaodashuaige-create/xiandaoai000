  • 与已解除连接的Meta/Facebook/Instagram资产关联、且没有合法保留理由的授权派生数据。
  • 可合理删除的账号配置、上传资料、历史操作记录或其他业务数据。

4. 可能保留的有限数据

若法律、税务、争议处理、安全、反欺诈或审计义务要求，我们可能在必要期限内保留最少量记录，期限结束后删除或匿名化。

5. 处理时间与确认

我们通常会在收到可验证的删除请求后尽快处理。一般目标是在30天内完成，或告知您需要额外时间的原因。处理完成后会通过您请求时使用的联系方式确认结果。

User Data Deletion Instructions

You may revoke LULIAN AI's access to third-party platforms at any time and request deletion of data associated with your account that is no longer needed.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Submit a Deletion Request

Email xiandao456@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my LULIAN AI data", or message us on WhatsApp at +60 10-651 9843.

To protect against unauthorized requests, we may ask for limited verification confirming your authority over the relevant business or workspace.

Security Reminder

Never send passwords, Access Tokens, App Secrets, verification codes, bank card details, or login credentials. None of these are needed to process a deletion request.

2. Revoke Meta / Facebook Access

  1. Sign in to the Facebook / Meta account used to authorize LULIAN AI.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy on Facebook / Meta.
  3. Open "Business Integrations" or "Apps and Websites" (naming may vary by region/version).
  4. Find "LULIAN AI Store Growth" and choose Remove / Revoke access.
  5. Once revoked, LULIAN AI can no longer access those assets. To also delete stored historical records inside LULIAN AI, submit Step 1 above.

3. What We Delete

  • Personal data associated with your LULIAN AI account or workspace that is no longer required to provide the service.
  • Authorization-derived data tied to disconnected Meta / Facebook / Instagram assets without a lawful retention basis.
  • Account settings, uploaded materials, historical logs, and other business records that can reasonably be deleted.

4. Limited Data That May Be Retained

Where required by law, tax, dispute resolution, security, anti-fraud, or audit obligations, we may retain minimal records for the required period before deletion or anonymization.

5. Timeline & Confirmation

We aim to complete verifiable deletion requests within 30 days, or inform you if additional time is required, and confirm completion via your contact channel.
