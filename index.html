Will AI spend by itself?

No. Strategy, creative, budget and launch are designed around approval boundaries. Prepared does not mean live.

Do I need Meta Ads expertise?

No. The product is designed around business questions: what you sell, where your customers are, what you want, and what budget you are comfortable with.

Can you guarantee ROI?

No. Results depend on market, offer, creative, budget, account history and follow-up. The product is designed to improve operating discipline and reduce avoidable manual work and testing waste.
