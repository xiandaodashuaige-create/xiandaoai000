Ask the LULIAN team
Is this an ad agency service or AI software?

LULIAN is designed as an AI advertising employee: software that connects account diagnosis, planning, creative, approvals, and advertising operations instead of selling a single image or recommendation. Book a demo to confirm the available setup and support scope.

Can I use it if I do not understand Meta Ads?

The product is designed around business questions such as what to promote, where customers are, how much to spend, and whether the ads are right. You do not need to learn media-buying jargon first. You still need legitimate access to the relevant Meta assets.

Will AI spend my ad budget by itself?

The product uses approval gates: review strategy, creative, and budget first. Creating an ad does not automatically launch it. Live delivery requires the relevant approval and depends on the enabled product scope. The interactive demo does not connect to an ad account or spend money.

Which platforms and markets are supported?

The product currently focuses on Facebook and Instagram advertising within the Meta ecosystem and related inquiry journeys, initially for beauty and local-service businesses in Hong Kong, Singapore, and Malaysia. WhatsApp is also our consultation channel. Account eligibility and supported ad formats are confirmed during the demo.

How is it priced, and are results guaranteed?

Contact us on WhatsApp for the suitable plan, service scope, and pricing. Media spend is separate from software or service fees. Results vary by industry, market, creative, budget, and follow-up, so fixed lead volume, sales, or ROI is not guaranteed.

Is the dashboard on this page showing live advertising data?

No. This is an interactive product-flow illustration, not your account diagnosis, a customer case, or live advertising data. Book a demo with the LULIAN team to verify available features and real operating status.
